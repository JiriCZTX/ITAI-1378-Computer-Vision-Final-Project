# Data

No data is committed to this repository. Everything below is downloaded into `data/raw/` (ignored by git) or generated by code.

**Rule:** only mock, synthetic and specimen documents. No real person's passport or ID is ever used, uploaded or stored.

## 1. Main source: MIDV-2020

| | |
|---|---|
| What | 1,000 unique mock identity documents: 100 each of 10 types, every one with unique fake text values, a mock signature and an artificially generated face |
| Types | ID cards of Albania (`alb_id`), Spain (`esp_id`), Estonia (`est_id`), Finland (`fin_id`), Slovakia (`svk_id`); passports of Azerbaijan (`aze_passport`), Greece (`grc_passport`), Latvia (`lva_passport`), Serbia (`srb_passport`); internal passport of Russia (`rus_internalpassport`) |
| Alphabets | Latin (with diacritics), Cyrillic, Greek |
| Images | 72,409 annotated: 1,000 templates, 1,000 smartphone photos (2268×4032), 1,000 upright + 1,000 rotated scans (2480×3507), 68,409 video frames (10 per second of 1,000 clips) |
| Capture conditions | low light, keyboard / table / cloth / text backgrounds, outdoors, strong perspective, glare (the image code tells which) |
| Paper | Bulatov K.B. et al., *MIDV-2020: A Comprehensive Benchmark Dataset for Identity Document Analysis*, Computer Optics 46(2), 2022 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.00396 |
| Download | ftp://smartengines.com/midv-2020/ (anonymous FTP, reachable on 2026-10-09; `md5.txt` there for checksums) · second mirror: University of La Rochelle sFTP, after the access form at https://l3i-share.univ-lr.fr/MIDV2020/midv2020.html |
| Terms | Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.5 (`license.txt` on the FTP server). Source images come from Wikimedia Commons; faces from Generated Photos, whose attribution is encouraged. Any use must cite the paper above. Nothing from the dataset is re-published in this repo |

### Labels (already exist, JSON readable with VGG Image Annotator)
- **Templates:** for every text field, its rectangle and its **exact value**; boxes for the holder photo, face and signature
- **Photos, scans, video frames:** the **4 document corners** (`doc_quad`, first corner = top-left, then clockwise) and the face box
- **MRZ lines** (`mrz_line0`, `mrz_line1`) on the 4 passport types that have them
- **Document type** = the folder name

### Labels I create or clean
- Field boxes on each photo and scan: mapped from the template through the 4 annotated corners (a homography). Scripted, then spot-checked on 50 images.
- Nothing is hand-labelled.

### What I use (≈13,000 images)
- All 1,000 photos and 2,000 scans
- 10 evenly spaced frames from each of the 1,000 video clips

### What I download (17.4 GB of 124 GB)
Sizes from the FTP server listing. Only 5 of the 8 archives are needed:

| Archive | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `templates.tar` | 0.86 GB | field values and boxes, mapped onto photos and scans |
| `photo.tar` | 4.00 GB | all 1,000 photos |
| `scan_upright.tar` | 1.15 GB | all 1,000 upright scans |
| `scan_rotated.tar` | 1.12 GB | all 1,000 rotated scans |
| `clips.tar` | 10.30 GB | annotated video frames; 10 kept per clip |
| `clips_video.tar`, `scan_upright_tif.tar`, `scan_rotated_tif.tar` | 106 GB | not downloaded (raw videos and TIFF copies of the scans) |

After keeping 10 frames per clip, the working set is about 9 GB. It fits on the Colab disk and in a free 15 GB Google Drive.

### Split: by document, never by image
All images of one mock document (photo, both scans, video frames) stay in the same split, so the test set has documents the models have never seen.

| Split | Documents | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Train | 700 (70 per type) | fine-tune the corner detector and the type classifier |
| Validation | 150 (15 per type) | choose thresholds, calibrate confidence |
| Test | 150 (15 per type) | final metrics only, touched once |

## 2. Synthetic MRZ lines (generated)
- 5,000 TD3 (passport) and TD1 (ID card) code lines for fictional people, with correct check digits
- Rendered in an OCR-B-style font with blur, glare, tilt, low light and JPEG compression
- Labels are known by construction; used to test the check-digit reader and the OCR repair rule

## 3. ICAO Doc 9303 specimens
- The official specimen lines of the fictional person *Anna Maria Eriksson* of *Utopia* (`UTO`)
- Used as conformance tests; see `notebooks/00_mrz_check_digits.ipynb` (all checks pass)

## 4. Unknown-type test: MIDV-500
- 50 document types, 500 video clips; public, also on Hugging Face (`Noaman/midv500`, MIT)
- The 40 types that are **not** in MIDV-2020 are never used for training; they test whether the system answers "unknown" instead of forcing a known type
